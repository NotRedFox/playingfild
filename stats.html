Reorder Tabs
Automatically reorders your open tabs by importance. A tab counts as important when you spend real time on it and when you keep coming back to it, so something you reopen all day sits left of something you left running once. Off means tabs stay wherever you put them.

Sorts tabs by importance so the ones you use and revisit most stay leftmost.

Auto group similar tabs

Keeps tabs about the same thing next to each other. Read five articles on one topic across five different sites and they gather into one run instead of scattering across the bar.

Matching uses page titles and site names, all on your device. It catches shared wording, so "Coronavirus" pages group together, but it will not spot that "COVID-19" means the same thing.

With Reorder Tabs on: groups are ordered by importance, and so are the tabs inside each group.
With Reorder Tabs off: nothing is promoted. Related tabs are pulled together and otherwise keep the order you left them in.

Tab Bar Ranking

Per tab: Each tab is tracked on its own. Tabs you use more move left; tabs you use less move right and close first when you hit your tab limit.

Per website: Time is pooled by website across all tabs. If you spend time on youtube.com in one tab, opening YouTube again in another tab shares that history for ordering and tab-limit closing.
